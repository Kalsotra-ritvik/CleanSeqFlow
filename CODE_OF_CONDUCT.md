# Code of conduct

This project is maintained by researchers for researchers. We want it to be a welcoming
place for people of every background and level of experience, including students asking
their first bioinformatics question.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give constructive and
specific feedback, credit other people's work, and accept correction gracefully.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or demeaning comments, discrimination of any kind,
sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information, and sustained
disruption of discussions.

**Scope:** all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and public spaces where
someone represents the project.

**Enforcement:** report problems privately to the maintainers (see the repository owner's
contact details on GitHub). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude participants who break these
rules.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
